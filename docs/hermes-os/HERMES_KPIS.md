# Hermes KPIs

## Command Center

* Blocked tasks
* Review-required tasks
* Completed tasks per week
* Strategic goals completed
* Cross-board throughput

## Engineering Lab

* Tests passing
* Bugs closed
* Features shipped
* Build success rate
* Deployment success rate
* Technical debt reduced

## Venture Portfolio

* Ideas evaluated
* Ventures researched
* Ventures validated
* MVPs planned
* MVPs launched
* Revenue-generating ventures
* Killed weak ideas

## Consulting Office

* Proposals sent
* SOWs completed
* Clients won
* Retainers created
* Monthly consulting revenue
* Gross margin

## Career Development

* Certifications completed
* Degree progress
* Applications submitted
* Interviews scheduled
* Offers received
* Salary growth

## Engineering Brand

* Videos published
* Articles published
* Projects showcased
* Subscribers/followers gained
* Portfolio depth

## Artist Management

* Collections planned
* Exhibitions targeted
* Collector contacts
* Artworks sold
* Average sale price
* Institutional progress

## KPI Rules

* Every profile must report progress weekly.
* Every major task should map to at least one KPI.
* If a task does not move a KPI, it should be questioned.
